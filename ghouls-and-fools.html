PROJECT 03 / TEAM DEVELOPMENT

Ghouls’n’Fools.

A one-level local multiplayer game made by a six-person team. Players cooperate to escape a haunted hotel, using flashlights and an upgradeable vacuum to deal with the ghosts.

MY ROLE
Team lead & gameplay programmer
CONTEXT
Team of 6
TECHNOLOGY
UNITY / C# / LOCAL CO-OP
Get the game on itch.io ↗ Watch the trailer ↗

01

My contribution

Alongside leading the team, I worked on the core gameplay loop, UI design and functionality, and making the game run smoothly. Programming was shared across the team: the Ghost AI, for example, was another team member’s work.

02

From an idea to a playable loop

Roughly half of my responsibilities involved taking mechanics the game designers had prototyped separately and bringing them together in the final game. That meant connecting the systems, resolving integration issues and refining how they worked together in the local co-op experience.

03

An experiment that did not ship

Early in development I experimented with a custom HLSL shader. It was ultimately scrapped, so it is part of the project’s development history rather than a feature of the released game.

04

A team result

The finished game reflects the work of the full team. The itch.io page includes the download, trailer and development logs, offering a broader view of how the project came together.
