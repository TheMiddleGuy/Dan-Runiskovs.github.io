PROJECT 01 / ENGINE DEVELOPMENT

Bomberman.

A Bomberman game built with my own SDL3-based C++ engine, developed and extended from Minigin. The project combines reusable engine systems with grid-based gameplay across Solo, Co-op and PvP modes.

MY ROLE
Engine & game programmer
CONTEXT
Individual project
TECHNOLOGY
C++ / SDL3 / MINIGIN
View source ↗

01

My contribution

I developed the engine and game, extending the Minigin foundation with systems for scenes, components, input, audio, state management, collisions, resources, events and save data.

02

A stack for menus and gameplay

The game-state stack manages the title screen, menus, gameplay, game-over screen and leaderboard. Push, Pop and Change operations allow transitions to be queued. This was one of my favourite parts of the project: a single structure supporting several distinct parts of the game.

03

My bit of optimisation

One small optimisation I’m particularly proud of is the leaderboard. I store scores in hundreds, save them alongside the player’s initials in binary, and reconstruct the displayed score in-game. Only the top five scores are kept, with a single qualification check at the end of each game. It’s a tiny tweak, but it still makes me happy. As a friend put it: This would make a Nintendo cartridge shed a tear of joy.

04

Choosing the right amount of complexity

Rectangle intersection tests provide lightweight collision detection. A compact binary format stores leaderboard entries. These choices fit the game’s requirements and kept the systems manageable. The final development stages were rushed; code cleanup and consistency remain areas I would improve.
