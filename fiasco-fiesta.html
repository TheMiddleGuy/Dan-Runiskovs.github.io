PROJECT 04 / GAME JAM

Fiasco Fiesta.

A local multiplayer game made by a six-person team during a three-day Xbox Game Camp Belgium game jam. Players organise a birthday party, unpack supplies and try to keep the children happy.

MY ROLE
Team lead & programmer
CONTEXT
Team of 6 · 3 days
TECHNOLOGY
UNITY / C# / LOCAL MULTIPLAYER
Get the game on itch.io ↗ View source ↗

01

My contribution

I was the team lead and one of the programmers on this Unity C# project. The game was a shared effort across programming, art and sound, completed within the three-day jam.

02

A small game built around cooperation

Up to four players share the party preparations: unpacking items, helping the children and keeping the party running. The short round puts the focus on coordination and chaotic local multiplayer.

03

Working within three days

The three-day deadline made this an intense project, but working with such a talented, dedicated and motivated team was a pleasure. Everyone brought something valuable, and I’m proud of what we managed to make together in such a short time.
