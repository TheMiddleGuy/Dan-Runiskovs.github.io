My background

My name is Danila Runiskovs, but almost everyone calls me Dan. I’m a student at Howest University of Applied Sciences, studying Digital Arts and Entertainment with a major in Game Development. Here, I learn about gameplay programming, engine architecture, data structures, algorithms and optimisation, mostly in C++ and C#. Along the way, I’ve also explored procedural modelling in Houdini, simulations and writing software for the Nintendo NES.

This isn’t my first experience with higher education. I previously attended ULB in Brussels, where I studied translation and interpretation and developed my language and social skills.

The past few years have taught me not to be afraid of the unfamiliar. With learning and practice, there’s no mountain I can’t climb.
